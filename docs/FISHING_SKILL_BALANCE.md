# Fishing skill balance verification

Freshly rerun on the reconstructed integration and final zero-threshold hazard guard on 2026-10-02. The earlier environment-reset report is archived separately under [docs/recovery](recovery/FISHING_SKILL_BALANCE_HISTORICAL.md); the evidence linked below was generated by this new run.

**Result: 18,040 actual GDScript fights, 341/341 regression assertions and all 9 balance gates passed.** Godot 4.6.3 completed in 191.65 seconds, with no engine errors or simulation-input changes during the run.

## Main findings

- Delayed visible-cue control caught 3,608/3,608 fish. Holding continuously caught none; blind one-second pull/release cycles caught 28.02%.
- Maximum-size fish took a median 104.33 seconds, versus 32.74 seconds for middle-size fish. Matched within-species/gear duration ratios were 3.874× maximum/minimum and 3.246× maximum/middle.
- Maximum-size visible-cue trials: 1,056/1,056 successes, with no premature wear warnings. Tension-only control caught 529/1056 (50.09%). Continuous hard pulling lost every maximum-size trial.
- All 264 visual-only hook trials succeeded after sustained float observations and a 200ms reaction. None struck during a nibble.
- Deliberate low-pressure stalemates produced visible warnings before occasional near-term wear failures: 2/16 seeds broke within 30 seconds after warning; the earliest warning-to-break interval was 23.95 seconds. Continuing to stall accumulated risk.

The matrix gives all fight policies the same legitimate hook before comparing battle skill. This avoids inflating blind-policy losses through early strikes. Early/late strikes and visual-only float detection have separate regressions.

The behavior-aware controller samples visible cues at 15Hz and queues responses for 170ms. Frame quantization yields approximately 170–267ms cue-to-input delay. It waits for surge-warning amplitude ≥0.15 or active-surge amplitude ≥0.08 instead of instantly reading an invisible phase transition. Current visible recovery pose and tension guide retrieval. It never consumes future phase timing, RNG, internal stamina targets or wear rolls. Tension-only control uses the same delay without fish/rod-motion observations.

## Frame-rate results and limits

Identical clock-aligned input replay produces exactly identical fight time, tension, distance, stamina, wear and RNG state at 16/30/60fps. The session consumes every elapsed second through fixed internal steps; it does not discard time at 16fps.

The realistic controller is sampled at each external frame rate, so its actual input edges differ slightly. All behavior-aware outcomes remained wins. Its successful-duration spread across frame rates was median 0.637 seconds, p90 4.750 seconds and maximum 10.283 seconds.

There were 52 borderline outcome disagreements: 34 tension-only cases and 18 metronome cases, with zero reactive disagreements. Among cases caught at all three frame rates, maximum successful-duration spreads were 19.933 seconds for tension-only and 21.825 seconds for metronome. Full individual cases are included in the JSON. These measurements do not support a blanket assertion that adaptive controller results are identical across frame rates.

## Float, input and lifecycle regressions

- 44 species × two seeds × 16/30/60fps: sustained dip/lift/drag observation, 100ms evidence accumulation and 200ms delayed strike; 264/264 correct hooks, zero nibble false positives
- WAITING and NIBBLE accept an early reel press and terminate an empty cast, without a later automatic hook from held input
- A missed strike expires without entering a fight
- No bite or nibble audio event emitted by the session
- One hook edge per press; release clears reel input; terminal catch event and catch identity remain unique
- Pause freezes tested timers, float observations, RNG and fight values; resume retains state and clears held reel input
- Charge cancellation rejects a late cast; background-style cancellation clears reeling; reset abandons the round
- A real caught round is unchanged after repeated input and duplicate finish calls

Rendered fish visibility, camera transform/FOV, button appearance and physical float transforms belong to the separate stage/UI suites. This report does not claim those rendered checks, Android certification or save-settlement integration.

## Organic stale-fight wear

Sixteen seeded maximum-size carp fights on gear 4 were deliberately maintained at low pressure using actual input edges. No test injected wear or selected an RNG result. The control pattern avoided slack escape while leaving the retrieve unfinished.

- Warning onset: 68.833–87.717 seconds; median 69.008 seconds
- Earliest warning-to-break interval: 23.950 seconds, exceeding the eight-second warning guard
- Within 30 seconds after warning: 2/16 breaks; this is a seeded sample rather than a population probability estimate
- Indefinite stalling accumulated risk; all 16 broke within the 500-second observation ceiling
- Active reactive fights: zero wear warnings or wear failures

Four blind metronome trials also ended in wear failure. Other blind losses included overload and prolonged slack. Fresh failure examples retain species, gear, size, seed, frame rate, duration and reason in the machine-readable summary.

## Zero-threshold boundary regression and capture continuity

A separate boundary probe found that an inverse-CDF RNG endpoint could produce a zero break threshold. The original unguarded terminal comparison then evaluated zero hazard ≥ zero threshold and ended a fresh fight after 1/120 second, with no warning. The actual failing probe and pre-guard matrix are retained in [pre-guard evidence](evidence/1.2.0-beta.2/recovered/pre_guard/README.md).

The final terminal condition now independently requires severe wear (>0.78) and at least eight seconds of warning before comparing hazard against its threshold. Seven focused boundary cases pass 28/28 assertions: fresh zero threshold, insufficient wear, absent or unfinished warning grace, warned eligible zero threshold, and positive thresholds below/at hazard. These explicitly injected boundary fixtures complement the organic seeded wear trials; they are not probability measurements.

The focused session-observation suite also passed 121/121 checks. After this one-line production fix, the complete 18,040-fight matrix and all 341 regressions were freshly rerun. Its raw seeded result bytes were identical to the actual reconstructed pre-guard run, and all runtime-input hashes remained unchanged during the final run.

The existing 19.1-second clip was compared against both actual session revisions using its ordinary seeded encounter, recorded 20fps input policy and 0.025-second substeps. Across 382 frames and 764 substeps there were zero old/new differences in state, float motion, tension, stamina, phase, warnings or RNG, and zero mismatches against the capture's recorded session observations.
